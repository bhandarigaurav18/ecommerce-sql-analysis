# Key Findings

## 1. Strong order completion

The dataset contains 5,000 orders:

- Completed: 4,163 (83.26%)
- Cancelled: 514 (10.28%)
- Returned: 323 (6.46%)

The completion rate is strong, while cancelled and returned orders are areas worth investigating.

## 2. Website is the strongest sales channel

Website generated the highest completed revenue:

- Website: $392,925.29
- Mobile App: $307,501.32
- Marketplace: $123,053.07

Website also had the highest number of completed orders (1,978).

Average order value was similar across channels, suggesting that the revenue difference was mainly driven by order volume.

## 3. Beauty generated the highest category revenue

Completed revenue by category:

- Beauty: $197,057.70
- Electronics: $190,414.94
- Home: $187,707.48
- Fashion: $124,183.48
- Sports: $124,116.08

Beauty generated the highest completed revenue.

Beauty also had the highest revenue per unit at $98.19, while Sports had the highest unit sales in the earlier category analysis.

## 4. Water Bottle was the top product by completed revenue

The top products by completed revenue were:

1. Water Bottle — $45,534.94
2. Body Lotion — $41,443.64
3. Gym Gloves — $40,242.37
4. Smart Watch — $38,558.51
5. Shampoo — $38,047.58

Water Bottle was the highest-revenue product, but the top products were relatively close to one another rather than being dominated by a single product.

## 5. Regular customers generated the most segment revenue

Completed revenue by customer segment:

- Regular: $326,568.31
- Loyal: $230,228.94
- New: $187,348.75
- At Risk: $79,333.68

Regular customers generated the highest total revenue because they represent the largest customer group.

Loyal customers remained important because of their higher purchase frequency and individual customer value.

## 6. Higher discounts were not associated with stronger sales

Higher discount levels were associated with lower sales volume and revenue in the dataset.

This does not prove that discounts reduce sales. Other factors, such as product demand and product mix, may influence the relationship.

## 7. Monthly revenue was relatively stable

Completed monthly revenue remained within a relatively narrow range during 2025.

The highest month was July at approximately $73,068.98, while the lowest was December at approximately $64,878.42.

This suggests relatively stable demand rather than a strong overall upward trend.

## 8. Overall business takeaway

The business shows strong order completion and relatively stable demand.

The main opportunities are:

- Protect the performance of the Website channel.
- Investigate cancelled and returned orders.
- Understand why Beauty generates higher revenue per unit.
- Retain and grow high-value Loyal customers.
- Test discount strategies rather than assuming larger discounts create stronger sales.

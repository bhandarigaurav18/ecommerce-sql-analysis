# Key Findings

## 1. Strong order completion
83.26% of orders were completed, while 10.28% were cancelled and 6.46% were returned. The 16.74% non-completion rate is an area for operational investigation.

## 2. Website is the strongest sales channel
The Website generated 392,925.29 in completed revenue from 1,978 completed orders. Mobile App generated 307,501.32 and Marketplace generated 123,053.07. Completed AOV was very similar across channels: 198.65 Website, 197.88 Mobile App, and 195.01 Marketplace. Therefore, the Website revenue lead is mainly driven by order volume.

## 3. Beauty leads category revenue, but Sports leads units
Beauty generated the highest category revenue at 250,858.14. Sports sold the most units at 2,508. Beauty also had the highest revenue per unit at 103.83, while Sports had the lowest at 63.28. This shows that sales volume alone does not determine revenue.

## 4. Loyal customers are the strongest individual segment
Regular customers generated the highest total segment revenue at 415,617.58 because they are the largest group. However, Loyal customers had the highest revenue per customer at 1,073.64 and the highest orders per customer at 5.21.

## 5. Discounts did not correspond to stronger sales in this dataset
Discount levels ranged from 0% to 20%. The 0% group had the highest units sold (5,399) and revenue (465,732.55), while the 20% group had 596 units and 42,641.40 revenue. This is an association, not proof that discounts reduce sales; product demand may influence which items receive discounts.

## 6. Revenue was not consistently growing during 2025
Monthly orders stayed relatively stable, mostly around 400–440. Revenue fluctuated, with July highest at 95,341.12 and December lowest at 82,093.73. Monthly AOV was highest in February at 222.19 and lowest in November at 195.00, suggesting lower order value contributed to weaker late-year revenue.

# Business Recommendations

## 1. Protect the Website channel

The Website generates the highest completed revenue and completed order volume.

The company should maintain the Website's performance while exploring ways to increase customer engagement and conversion on the Mobile App.

## 2. Investigate cancelled and returned orders

16.74% of orders were either cancelled or returned.

The company should investigate the main reasons behind these orders, such as payment issues, delivery problems, product expectations, or customer behavior.

## 3. Focus on high-performing categories

Beauty generated the highest completed revenue and the highest revenue per unit.

The company should investigate which products and customer groups are driving this performance and consider whether similar strategies can be applied to other categories.

## 4. Retain Loyal customers

Loyal customers represent an important high-value segment because they purchase frequently and generate strong revenue per customer.

Retention strategies such as personalized offers, loyalty rewards, and relevant product recommendations could help increase their long-term value.

## 5. Test discount strategies

Higher discounts were not associated with stronger sales in this dataset.

Instead of automatically increasing discounts, the company should test targeted promotions and measure their effect on conversion, order value, and revenue.

## 6. Monitor monthly performance

Monthly completed revenue was relatively stable throughout 2025.

The company should continue monitoring monthly revenue and average order value to identify meaningful changes in customer demand or purchasing behavior.

## 7. Improve decision-making with further analysis

The next step would be to investigate:

- Why customers cancel or return orders
- Why Beauty generates higher revenue per unit
- What makes Loyal customers more valuable
- Which products are frequently purchased together
- Whether customer acquisition channels produce different customer values

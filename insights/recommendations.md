# Business Recommendations

1. **Protect and improve the Website channel**
   - The Website is the largest completed-order and revenue channel.
   - Monitor conversion, customer experience, and availability there before shifting resources elsewhere.

2. **Investigate cancelled and returned orders**
   - 16.74% of orders were cancelled or returned.
   - Break this down by product, category, channel, customer segment, and payment method to identify operational causes.

3. **Use category-specific strategy**
   - Beauty has strong revenue and revenue per unit.
   - Sports has high unit volume but lower revenue per unit.
   - Consider different merchandising, pricing, and bundle strategies rather than evaluating categories only by units sold.

4. **Focus retention efforts on Loyal customers**
   - Loyal customers show the highest revenue per customer and order frequency.
   - Retention and repeat-purchase programs are logical areas to test.

5. **Do not assume bigger discounts create more sales**
   - Higher discount levels were not associated with higher sales in this dataset.
   - Test discounts by product and customer group rather than applying larger discounts broadly.

6. **Investigate the late-year revenue decline**
   - Order volume remained fairly stable, but AOV weakened toward year-end.
   - Analyze product mix, discounts, and customer behavior in the weaker months.
